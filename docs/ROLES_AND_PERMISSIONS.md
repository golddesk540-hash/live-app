# Popo Live — 1.1 Product Definition & 8 Core System Roles

## Product Overview
Popo Live is a real-time interactive live video and audio streaming platform. It connects audiences with live broadcasters, enables group voice/video party rooms, competitive host-versus-host PK battles, virtual gifting, gamified social progression, and agency management.

---

## 8 System Roles & Permissions Matrix

### 1. Visitor (Unauthenticated Guest)
- **Permissions:**
  - View public marketing website pages (Home, About, News, Policies, Agency info).
  - Switch between English and Urdu.
  - Preview select public live streams in read-only web view (limited duration or preview mode).
  - View App Store / Google Play download links.
- **Restrictions:**
  - Cannot chat, gift, follow, or create streams.

### 2. Normal User (Viewer / Fan)
- **Permissions:**
  - Watch public live streams and party rooms with unlimited duration.
  - Send text chat and free stickers in rooms.
  - Recharge wallet with coins via authorized payment gateways.
  - Send animated virtual gifts to hosts.
  - Request to join party room guest seats (subject to host approval).
  - Post pictures and text updates on Square.
  - Follow creators and join Host Fan Clubs.
  - Send 1-on-1 direct messages to followed users.
- **Restrictions:**
  - Cannot broadcast solo live without meeting eligibility gates (Face verification + Level 5 wealth gate).

### 3. Creator / Host
- **Permissions:**
  - All Normal User capabilities.
  - Create Solo Live broadcasts (video/audio) once Level 5 and face check are passed.
  - Create 9-seat Party Rooms with full microphone and seat management.
  - Initiate and accept PK Battles with other active streamers.
  - Receive 70% share of virtual gifts converted directly into withdrawable Host Points.
  - Complete daily streaming tasks and claim milestone streak rewards.
  - Request earnings withdrawals according to payment schedules.
  - Moderate own room (mute viewers, kick disruptive users, assign room assistants).
- **Restrictions:**
  - Must comply with streaming safety policies and age restrictions (18+).

### 4. Agency Admin
- **Permissions:**
  - Manage a dedicated agency portal.
  - Invite, sign, and onboard creators/hosts under agency management.
  - View aggregate streaming performance, hours, and earnings of affiliated hosts.
  - Receive agency revenue share commissions directly into agency wallet.
  - Submit agency-level payout requests.
- **Restrictions:**
  - No access to system-level moderation, user bans, or platform financial reserves.
  - Second-level agent commission is strictly disabled.

### 5. Super Admin
- **Permissions:**
  - Full, unrestricted access to the Admin Panel.
  - Manage all platform configuration settings, economy ratios, and feature flags.
  - Assign and revoke staff roles (Moderator, Finance, Support).
  - Final approval on high-value financial payouts and agency partnerships.
  - System-wide ban / unban authority across all devices and IPs.

### 6. Live Safety Moderator
- **Permissions:**
  - Real-time live room monitoring console with instant video grid preview.
  - One-click live stream kill-switch (terminate non-compliant broadcasts immediately).
  - Issue temporary mutes, room kicks, and user account suspensions.
  - Review flagged content in the user report queue.
  - Enforce Child Safety protocol (zero tolerance, immediate escalate-and-ban).
- **Restrictions:**
  - No access to financial payout management or banking details.

### 7. Finance Manager
- **Permissions:**
  - Manage coin top-up transaction logs and dispute resolutions.
  - Review and approve Creator & Agency withdrawal requests against fraud checks.
  - Authorize and monitor Coin Resellers and bulk coin distributions.
  - Export financial ledger, tax, and revenue reports.
- **Restrictions:**
  - No authority to alter safety moderation policies or live stream permissions.

### 8. Customer Support Agent
- **Permissions:**
  - View and resolve tickets in the Help & Feedback queue.
  - Assist users with account recovery, PIN resets, and basic profile issues.
  - View user moderation history and public activity logs.
- **Restrictions:**
  - Read-only on financial ledgers; cannot issue refunds or direct point adjustments without supervisor approval.
